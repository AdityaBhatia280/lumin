Project Lumin: Empowering young people to safely enjoy the outdoors.

Project Lumin is a student-led sun-safety awareness initiative based in Frisco, Texas. Our mission is to help young people build healthier everyday habits through awareness, community outreach, and student-led change.

who we are.

Helping young people build lifelong sun-safe habits.

Project Lumin is a student-led initiative based in Frisco, Texas. We make sun-safety awareness approachable and encourage young people to enjoy the outdoors with healthier everyday habits.
